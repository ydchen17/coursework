# 📘 Coursework

This repository collects selected coursework, assignments, and self-directed projects from my academic training and professional development. It spans degree programmes in health policy, data science, and biomedical sciences, as well as independent study and technical exploration.

> **Note**: This repository is **incomplete** and may be missing data or files due to archival limitations. It is maintained primarily for personal reference.

## 🎓 Degree Programmes

- **LSE coursework**: Selected assignments from the MSc in International Health Policy at the London School of Economics (LSE).
- **NTU coursework**: Coursework and projects from the MSc in Data Science at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.
- **ZJE coursework**: Materials from my undergraduate biomedical sciences programme at the Zhejiang University – University of Edinburgh Institute.
- **HP407 meta-analysis project**: An updated replication of a published meta-analysis, completed for the LSE course *HP407 Evidence Review and Synthesis for Decision-Making*.
- **NTU entry test**: Preparatory materials and practice scripts for NTU’s entry test.
- **Writing samples**: Examples of academic and policy writing, including essays and short research notes.

## 🧠 Self-Study

- **MOOCs**: Exercises and mini-projects from online courses related to data science, Python, R, and statistical modelling.
- **Raspberry Pi**: Experiments and code for hardware interfacing, data collection, and sensor integration using Raspberry Pi.
